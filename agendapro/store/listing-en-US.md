## Full description (up to 4000) · App Store and Google Play

```
AgendaPro is the complete scheduling app for Brazilian beauty, wellness and cleaning professionals working in the United States. The app is in Brazilian Portuguese, and the messages you send to clients can go out in Portuguese, English or Spanish.

Hair stylists, nail techs, estheticians, lash artists, massage therapists, house cleaners and personal trainers: your clients book on their own through your page, 24/7, and you run everything from your phone.

FREE 14-DAY TRIAL
Create your account in the app and use every feature for 14 days, no credit card. Then pick the plan that fits.

YOUR DAY AT A GLANCE
• "Today" screen: who is coming, when, and how much you will make
• Day and week calendar, manual bookings, reschedule and cancel
• Phone notification when a new booking comes in
• Send appointments to your iPhone or Android calendar
• Working hours, days off and vacations

ONLINE BOOKING PAGE
• Your own link showing only open time slots
• Link and QR code to share online and print for your front desk
• Automatic email confirmation and 24-hour reminder for clients
• Deposits by Zelle or cash (and by card on the Pro plan) to cut no-shows

CLIENTS
• Client profile with history, total spent, notes and birthday
• Ready-to-send WhatsApp messages: confirm, remind, running late, ask for a review
• Messages in English and Spanish for American and Latino clients (Pro)
• Recurring clients: weekly, every two weeks or monthly, and the calendar fills itself (Pro)
• Win-back list: see who has not come back in weeks and invite them again (Pro)
• Waitlist: when someone cancels, notify who was waiting (Pro)
• Star reviews and a photo gallery on your page (Pro)

MONEY UNDER CONTROL
• Mark paid, payment method and tip
• Expenses, monthly profit, goals and a tax reserve (Pro)
• Business mileage log for your tax deduction (Pro)
• Monthly report, CSV file for your accountant and branded PDF receipts (Premium)

FOR CLEANING PROFESSIONALS
• Recurring houses with address and notes for each home
• Turnovers: paste the home's calendar (.ics) from Airbnb, Vrbo or Booking.com and the cleaning shows up on checkout day
• Teams with their own color on the calendar and a daily route link, no password needed (Premium)

SECURITY AND PRIVACY
• Unlock with Face ID or fingerprint
• Same account as the BrasilConnect website, always in sync
• Delete your account anytime, right in the app

PLANS
• Starter (US$19/month): calendar, booking page, clients, email reminders, message templates, phone notifications, calendar sync, Zelle deposits, QR code and turnovers for up to 3 homes
• Pro (US$39/month): everything in Starter + card deposits, reviews, gallery, recurring clients, win-back list, waitlist, finances, mileage and English/Spanish messages
• Premium (US$79/month): everything in Pro + teams of up to 10 professionals, daily route, reports, PDF receipts and a booking page without BrasilConnect branding

Subscriptions are purchased on brasilconnectusa.com. No per-booking fees. Cancel anytime. If your plan is inactive, you can still see your calendar and clients.

Questions or ideas: oi@brasilconnectusa.com

Airbnb, Vrbo, Booking.com and WhatsApp are trademarks of their respective owners. AgendaPro is not affiliated with these companies.
```
